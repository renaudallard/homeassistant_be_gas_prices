# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Atrias | The Belgian market's clearing house; publishes the monthly gross calorific value of every gas reception station. |
| Calorific value (CBW, PCS, GCV) | kWh released by one normal cubic metre of gas: the factor that turns a metered volume into billed energy, per reception station and month. About 11,3 to 11,8 kWh/m³(n) since the switch to high-calorific gas in 2024. |
| Card, tariff card | The PDF (or page) a supplier publishes each month with its prices, the DSO tables and the levies. |
| Card archive | The project's daily copy of every card parsed, in the be_price_cards repository under `gas/`. Installations read it for months a supplier no longer serves. |
| CWaPE, VNR (formerly VREG), Brugel, CREG | The Walloon, Flemish, Brussels and federal energy regulators. |
| DSO | Distribution network operator: Fluvius (8 areas) in Flanders, ORES and RESA in Wallonia, Sibelga in Brussels. |
| EGSI | EEX's European Gas Spot Index, the day-ahead and weekend assessment many variable contracts are indexed on. |
| Energy contribution (bijdrage op de energie, cotisation sur l'énergie) | A federal levy on residential gas, zero since 1 August 2026. |
| Excise (bijzondere accijns, accise spéciale) | The federal special excise, billed per slice of the annual volume: up to 12 000 kWh and above. |
| Fluxys | The transmission system operator; its transport tariff is on every card. |
| GOS | Geaggregeerd ontvangstation: a gas reception station, whose calorific value applies to the connections it feeds. |
| Heren (ICIS Heren) | A price assessment service; Engie's and Eneco's indices are Heren's. |
| Index period | The period an index value covers: the delivery month, or a quarter. |
| Month-ahead (ZTP101, TTF101) | The average of month-ahead quotes taken in the month before delivery; known when the delivery month starts. |
| OSP levy | The Brussels per-meter levy that funds public-service obligations, by meter caliber. |
| Provisional | An indexed month's price its index may still move: at the value published for an earlier month while its own is not out, or at a card's printed price that is not final for that month. |
| RLP | Synergrid's residential load profile; an RLP-weighted index weights each day by how much a household draws. |
| Signing month, signing cohort | The month a contract was signed; its card's formula and fee bind the contract. |
| Tier (T1, T2, T3) | The distribution tariff's consumption tiers: up to 5 000, 150 000 and 1 000 000 kWh a year. One tier prices all of a year's kWh. |
| TTF, ZTP | The Dutch and Belgian gas trading hubs. |
| Walloon connection fee (redevance de raccordement) | 0,0075 c EUR/kWh on Walloon gas, VAT exempt. |
