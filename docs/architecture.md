## What the integration does

It publishes what gas costs a Belgian household, all in: the price of one kWh
and of one cubic metre as the bill prices it today, the yearly fixed costs,
and, from the household's gas meter, the running bill of the year and of the
month, the cost of a rolling year and a projection to 31 December.

A Belgian gas bill fuses three sources:

1. The supplier's energy price, from its own published tariff card: a fixed
   price, or a formula on a market index of the delivery month.
2. The regulated network tariff of the household's distribution network
   operator (DSO), which every card reprints: a distribution tier chosen by
   the annual volume, transport and metering.
3. The federal and regional levies: the special excise (billed per slice of
   the annual volume), the energy contribution, the Walloon connection fee
   and the Brussels per-meter levy. VAT is 6% on all of it except the Walloon
   connection fee.

The rule that shapes the codebase comes from the electricity integration: no
supplier price is ever typed into Python source. Every figure comes from a
live card. The few regulated figures in `const.py` are set by law or by the
regulator for everyone they apply to, carry the months they are known to
cover and expire into "read the card" past them.

## The module map

Paths relative to `custom_components/be_gas_prices/`.

| Module | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| `__init__.py` | Entry setup and teardown, the `refresh` and `backfill_statistics` services, the automatic price-history backfill. |
| `coordinator.py` | `GasCoordinator`: one tick fetches or keeps the card, refreshes index and calorific values, reads the meter and builds `CoordinatorData`. Persists its state in a Store. |
| `coordinator_data.py` | `CoordinatorData`, the record every entity reads, and `Conversion`. |
| `bill.py` | One month billed: the card, the energy leg (the month's card, the signing card or typed figures), the index value of the month, the volume and the days. Pure. |
| `running_costs.py` | The year walked month by month on each month's card, the rolling year and the year-end projection. Pure. |
| `pricing.py` | The per-kWh breakdown and the yearly fixed costs of one household on one card. Pure. |
| `gas_meter.py` | Finding the gas meter (configured or from the Energy dashboard) and reading its daily consumption out of recorder statistics, in m³ or kWh. |
| `calorific.py` | Atrias's monthly gross calorific value per gas reception station: the kWh one cubic metre is worth. |
| `month_cards.py` | The card of a past month: the supplier's archive, then the card archive in be_price_cards. `current_card`: the card a contract is priced on now, the card archive's OCR reading standing in for a card published as images. |
| `contract_periods.py` | Contracts the household held earlier in the year, recorded at a switch, billed on their own suppliers' cards. |
| `manual_rate.py` | Figures the household typed from its own contract, laid over the signing card. |
| `compare.py`, `compare_table.py`, `daily_ranking.py` | Quoting other contracts for the household, the ranked table, and the daily ranking behind `potential_saving`. |
| `backfill.py` | The price sensors' hourly statistics back to 1 January. |
| `snapshot_codec.py` | A snapshot as JSON and back, shared by the Store, the month cache and the card archive. |
| `issues.py`, `repairs.py` | The Repairs cards and the retry fix flow. |
| `postcodes.py`, `postcode_map.py` | Postcode to gas DSO, the map generated by `scripts/refresh_postcodes.py`. |
| `config_flow.py` | The setup wizard and the options flow. |
| `sensor.py`, `button.py`, `diagnostics.py` | The entities and the diagnostics download. |
| `const.py` | Keys, DSO ids, tier bounds and the regulated figures with their windows. |
| `providers/` | One module per supplier plus the shared framework; see [provider-framework.md](provider-framework.md). |

## The domain model

```
region  (flanders | wallonia | brussels)
  |
  +-- gas DSO   8 Fluvius areas | ores (one tariff for its 5 sub-areas) | resa | sibelga
  |
  +-- supplier  (a registry entry, providers/__init__.py)
        |
        +-- contract   kind fixed | variable | indexed
```

A household adds: its annual volume (typed, then measured), which picks the
distribution tier and the excise slices; in Brussels its meter caliber; its
gas meter; how a cubic metre becomes kWh; and optionally the contract's
signing month and dates.

## End-to-end data flow

```
config entry
    |
    v
async_setup_entry                       __init__.py
    GasCoordinator(hass, entry)
    async_load_persistent()             last card, index table, calorific values, month cards
    async_config_entry_first_refresh()
        |
        v
    _tick()                             coordinator.py
        card:      probe / 24 h TTL -> extractor.fetch()     providers/<supplier>.py
                   on failure: keep the card; if none or stale, the card archive
        index:     extractor.fetch_index() twice a day        (IndexedRates)
        calorific: Atrias GCV of the reception station daily  calorific.py
        months:    past months' cards, supplier archive then card archive
        meter:     recorder daily changes, m³ or kWh           gas_meter.py
        bills:     running_costs() -> bill_month() -> pricing.compute_breakdown()
        -> CoordinatorData
    forward platforms: sensor, button
    background: price history backfill                         backfill.py
```

## Freshness and fallbacks, at a glance

- The coordinator ticks hourly. A supplier with a cheap freshness key (a
  HEAD `Content-MD5`, an ETag) is probed every tick; any other card is
  fetched again after 24 hours, and on every tick once its month is over
  until the next one is out.
- A card that cannot be fetched never stops the pricing: the last good one
  keeps serving, from the Store across a restart. With no card at all, or a
  stale one (a week without a successful fetch, or a week past the month it
  prices), the card archive's latest row stands in.
- Index values are fetched twice a day, calorific values daily, both kept in
  the Store.
- A month whose own card cannot be read is billed on the current card and
  named in the year cost's `months_on_current_card` attribute.

## Adding a supplier

1. Add `providers/<supplier>.py` exposing `EXTRACTOR`, following
   `providers/engie.py`: contracts, `fetch`, `parse_snapshot`, and where the
   supplier offers them `fetch_for_month`, `fetch_index` and `probe`.
2. Register it in `providers/__init__.py`.
3. Add real cards under `tests/fixtures/<supplier>/` and a test module that
   pins the figures read off them, including the formula check (the formula
   at the index value the card names reproduces the printed price).
4. Add `docs/providers/<supplier>.md`.

The live check and the card archive walk the registry and need no change.
